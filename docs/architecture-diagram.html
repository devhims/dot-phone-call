How Dot Phone Call fits together

Dot asks for a call through the Dot Phone Local plugin, whose tools run on your Mac behind an OpenAI tunnel. The backend queues it, Twilio connects your phone to a hidden browser client, and BlackHole carries Dot's voice and yours between Dot's tab and that client, without touching your Mac's speakers or microphone.

Not drawn: the menu-bar app's supervisor, which starts the backend and the tunnel client on your Mac and restarts them if they crash; the extension also reports call progress to the backend. Incoming calls reach Twilio first, which asks for your PIN before ringing the hidden page.
